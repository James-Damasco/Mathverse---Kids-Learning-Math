# VERSION 3.1.0

- **Auto Carrie 1 Visualizer:** A live vertical column arithmetic assistant. It animates column overflows

(e.g., carrying a $1$ over to the tens column in addition)

or borrowing (lending a $10$ from left to right in subtraction) using clear vertical alignments:

\begin{array}{r@{\quad}|}
\text{➀} & \text{(Carry)} \\
18 & \\
+\ 15 & \\
\hlinr
33 &
\end{array}

- **Space Technique & Tricks Hub:** Interactive conceptual cheat sheets to master mental arithmetic, factoring reversals, and distributive splitting (e.g., solving $12$ $x$ $5$ by partitioning it into $(10 x 5 ) + (2 x 5) = 60$.

- **P2P Arithmetic Duels:** Real-time cloud matching rooms allowing kids to challenge siblings, classmates, or our custom-engineered ``CosmicAI Speed Bots`` in rapid-fire calculations duels.

- **Global Star Leaderboards:** An online champion arena that encourages friendly competition by tracking total stars earned across the cosmos.

- **Gemini AI Diagnostic Engine:** A seamless report generator that converts local gameplay telemetry (accuracy rates, active streaks, times spent) into high-fidelity pedagogical advice and customizable action plans for parents.

- **PWA Standalone Cache & Offline Sync:** Automatic Service Worker sync that transitions seamlessly between cloud-saved Firestore data and offline local storage.
