# V.3.2.0: SPACE GUILDS & CLAN MISSIONS

**Target Release:** November 2026

- **Engagement Focus:** Teamwork and Collaborative Social Learning.

**Gameplay Mechanics:**
- Classroom Guilds: Students can join forces to tackle massive "Cosmic Bosses" (e.g., solving $500$ colelctive questions in $24$ hours.)
- Shared Star Banks: Earn collective points to purchase decorative badges, solar flags, and customized spaceships for the shared guild hanger.
